Test Three: 

run "make testThree"

A partition should not be assigned after a split is made if it cannot properly serve as a usable
space. This means the left over partition needs to be of the size of a header, footer, and the 
assigned space needs to be aligned on the 4-byte boundry, so must be 4 bytes. These requirements
add up to 12 bytes. If the new partition cannot fulfill this amount, then the allocation must
occupy the space of the partition.

malloc also needs to indicate when an allocation fails, namely when there is not enough space
fulfill the allocation. this test will have an allocation of almost the entire heap, where
less than 12 bytes will remain, forcing the allocation to occupy the partition, and then 
another malloc will be called. Since there will be no room left, the ptr to the header will
be assigned NULL and the user will be notified of such.

- malloc of size 65520 is called
- since 8 bytes will remain, the allocation will occupy the space of the would be partition
- another malloc is called
- since there is no space left on the heap, NULL will be returned
